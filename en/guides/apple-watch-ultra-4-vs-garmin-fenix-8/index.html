Apple Watch Ultra 4 vs Garmin fēnix 8.

Two very different premium approaches: Apple focuses on smart integration and health; Garmin on battery life, maps and multisport depth.

Criterion
Apple Watch Ultra 4
Garmin fēnix 8
Battery life
Up to 50 hours daily use; 84 hours in Low Power Mode.
From days to weeks depending on version; the 47 mm AMOLED model reaches up to 16 days in smartwatch mode.
Sport & GPS
Advanced GPS, new sensors and Readiness score.
Multiband GPS, preloaded maps, Training Readiness and extensive multisport profiles.
Ecosystem
Deep integration with iPhone, Apple Health and Apple services.
Garmin Connect with broad iOS and Android compatibility.
Daily use
More focused on full smartwatch features, apps and Apple integration.
More focused on outdoor, endurance, navigation and long battery life.

How to choose

If you want a full smartwatch inside the Apple ecosystem, Ultra 4 is the more natural fit. If you want much longer battery life, maps and deep endurance tools, fēnix 8 remains a very strong option.
