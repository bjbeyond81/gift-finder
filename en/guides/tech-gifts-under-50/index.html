Tech gifts · budget

Tech gifts under €50 that make sense.

There is plenty of tech under €50, but also plenty of noise. Focus on clear use cases, compatibility and everyday value.

Start with the use case

Think about what the person will actually do with the product. Audio, charging and smartphone accessories usually outperform novelty gadgets.

Check compatibility

USB-C, Lightning, Android, iPhone and charging standards can decide whether a gift is useful or frustrating.

Ignore specs without context

More features do not automatically mean better value. Reliability and simplicity matter more.
