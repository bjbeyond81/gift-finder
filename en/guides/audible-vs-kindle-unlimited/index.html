Audible vs Kindle Unlimited.

They are not direct competitors: one is built around listening, the other around digital reading.

Criterion
Audible
Kindle Unlimited
Format
Audiobooks, podcasts and audio content.
eBooks and digital reading selections.
Best use
Travel, walking, driving and screen-free moments.
Reading on Kindle, smartphone, tablet or the Kindle app.
Free trial
30 days for eligible customers; Audible specifies conditions and automatic renewal.
30 days for eligible customers; then renewal under Amazon terms.
Best for
People who listen more than they read.
Frequent readers who want to explore many titles.

How to choose

Choose Audible if you want stories while doing something else. Choose Kindle Unlimited if you prefer reading and want a broad catalog across devices.
