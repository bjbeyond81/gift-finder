Prime vs Prime Student.

Prime Student is designed for eligible students; the key issue is not the plan name but whether you meet the requirements and which terms are currently active.

Criterion
Amazon Prime
Prime Student
Audience
Amazon customers in general.
Eligible students under Amazon requirements.
Benefits
Includes the Prime benefits provided by the plan.
Includes Prime benefits with student-specific terms.
Promotions
Free trials may be available for eligible customers.
Longer student-specific promotional periods may be available, subject to Amazon eligibility.
Practical choice
If you are not a student or are not eligible.
If you are an eligible student and want student-specific terms.

How to choose

Always check Amazon before signing up: trial length, renewal price and eligibility can change. Prime Student only makes sense if you actually meet the eligibility criteria.
