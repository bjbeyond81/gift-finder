Wearables · buying guide

How to choose a smartwatch.

Choose around your real daily use. Battery life, GPS, compatibility and comfort usually matter more than the total feature count.

Battery life

Longer battery life matters for training, travel and continuous health tracking.

GPS and training

Runners and cyclists should focus on GPS quality, heart-rate stability, mapping and training support.

Compatibility

Check phone, operating system, app support and ecosystem limitations before buying.

Comfort

Weight, case size and strap matter because a watch only helps if you actually wear it.
